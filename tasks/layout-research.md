# Workspace Layout Reference Findings

## Sources reviewed

1. LumApps, *Intranet Design: Best Practices & 5 Examples* — https://www.lumapps.com/insights/blog/intranet-design
2. Origami Connect, *6 Company Intranet Examples to Impress Your Employees in 2026* — https://www.origamiconnect.com/blog/company-intranet-examples

## Applied findings

The proposed designs retain a clear, task-oriented information architecture; make important announcements visibly distinct; place search and common routes near the top; and preserve mobile access to the same core information.

For **Option A**, the requested animated twin-feed presentation should pause on hover and be accessible through manual controls. The independent, fixed utility column will hold a compact calendar, the current index, and upcoming activity/announcement cards.

For **Option B**, the recommended direction is a stable, user-controlled editorial dashboard: one onboarding feature carousel, a prioritized news grid, and a fixed utility rail. This follows the reviewed pattern of reducing noise through targeted content rather than continuously moving every information surface.
